# Public-Dancer Promo Video — Creative Brief (W-6)

**Status:** brief only. Production has not started and is not authorized. The marketing campaign hold applies: this piece is not scheduled for advertising or social posting.
**Owner decisions:** 2026-10-22 two-audience website (see `docs/roadmap/DanceFlow_Master_Roadmap.md`, Phase 3, WEB-2A).

## 1. Purpose
Give public dancers a short, warm reason to use DanceFlow Discover. It is the dancer-side counterpart to the ARIA commercial, which speaks to dance businesses. The video should leave a first-time dancer thinking "there's a place for me, and it's easy to find."

## 2. Audience
- **Primary:** adults curious about social or partner dancing (beginners, people returning to dance, couples, people new to a city).
- **Secondary:** current dancers looking for classes, socials, events, practice partners or a new studio.
- **Not the audience:** competitive or professional dancers. This piece is not about competitions, results or performance.

## 3. Format
- **Length:** 30–45 seconds, delivered as a horizontal 16:9 master. A 9:16 vertical cut and a 15-second cutdown can follow later.
- **Delivery:** viewer-started (no autoplay), English captions (WebVTT), readable without sound (on-screen text carries the message), safe areas respected for the vertical cut.
- **Hosting:** the same model as the ARIA videos. The owner uploads the approved file and sets an https MP4 URL in the environment; nothing is committed to Git.

## 4. Message
- **Core line (draft, owner to approve):** "Find your place in dance."
- **Supporting idea:** studios, classes, events and dance partners near you, all in one place.
- **Closing:** the DanceFlow logo, "Explore Discover", and the URL `idanceflow.com/discover`.

## 5. Story arc (about 40 s)
| Beat | Time | Picture | On-screen text / VO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curiosity | 0–6 s | An adult watching a social dance, or scrolling on a phone at home | "Always wanted to dance?" |
| Search | 6–14 s | Discover on a phone: studios, then a beginner-friendly filter, then a class with open spots (real product UI from DEV or synthetic data, no real customer data) | "Find studios and classes near you." |
| First step | 14–24 s | Arriving at a studio, being welcomed, a first lesson with an instructor | "Book an intro lesson or join a class." |
| Belonging | 24–34 s | A relaxed group social: partners rotating, laughing, mixed ages | "Meet people. Find a partner. Keep dancing." |
| Close | 34–40 s | Logo and end card | "Find your place in dance. Explore Discover." |

## 6. Visual and tone direction
- Social partner dancing with everyday adults: varied ages, body types and backgrounds, casual clothing, real studio and social-floor settings. No competition costumes, rhinestones, judging, podiums or ballroom-competition staging.
- Warm and welcoming rather than glossy. Natural light, genuine smiles, and imperfect beginners are good.
- Brand palette accents (`#5b145e` plum, `#d88a2d` amber, `#fff9f3` cream) for text cards and the end card. The logo is from `public/brand/logo/` (Candidate A).
- Product UI shots must use real, shipped Discover screens (Studios, Classes, Events, Dance partner listings). Do not show unshipped features.

## 7. Claims restrictions (same as the public-copy guard)
- Dance partners are **listings**, never "matching", "compatible" or "algorithm".
- No Featured Events or promoted-placement claims.
- No competition judging, scoring or results claims.
- No studio counts or city-coverage claims unless verified at the time of production.

## 8. Placement (when approved for publishing)
- **Homepage:** the dancer section ("A public home for the dance community."), as a secondary player beside the Discover paths.
- **`/discover` hub:** hero area, below the headline.
- Never autoplay; the homepage player uses `preload="none"`.

## 9. Production inputs still needed (owner)
- Approval of the core line and story arc.
- Footage source: an owned shoot at a partner studio (with signed releases) or licensed stock that fits the photography direction.
- Voice: none (text-led), or a warm narrator distinct from ARIA's voice.
- Music: an upbeat social-dance feel, licensed for web and social use.

## 10. Out of scope
Producing, editing or publishing the video; ad buys; social posts; email campaigns.
