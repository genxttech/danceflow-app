# ConfiDance texting setup: instructions for the authorized representative

Status: DRAFT for the DanceFlow owner to share. Nothing has been sent to anyone. Nothing here is collected or entered by DanceFlow engineering or by Claude.

## Who does this
One person who has authority to act for ConfiDance Unlimited, LLC (for example an owner, director or general manager) and who signs in to the DanceFlow app as the **studio owner of ConfiDance Studio**. Only the studio owner can submit this form. Do not hand the EIN or any of these details to anyone else to type in for you.

## Before you start (about 10 minutes)
- Your IRS EIN letter (CP 575 or 147C) in front of you. The legal business name must match it exactly; it is often different from the studio's display name.
- The business's street address, and the website you want shown (your own site, or the DanceFlow page).
- The representative's name, job title, business email and mobile number.
- A preferred area code for the studio's texting number (3 digits).

## Steps (Settings > Texting)
1. **Business.** Legal business name (exactly as on the IRS letter), business type, industry (Education or Consumer), EIN, website choice and address. The EIN is encrypted the moment you save it and is never shown again, only its last four digits. If a save is rejected, retype the EIN: it is deliberately never sent back to the page.
2. **Representative.** The authorized person's first and last name, job title, position, business email and mobile number.
3. **Texting number.** Your preferred area code. DanceFlow picks a local number for you; you cannot choose a vanity number.
4. **Review.** Read the generated description of what your clients agree to, the sample messages and the three public pages (SMS Terms, SMS Privacy, opt-in form). Tick the four statements only if each is true:
   - I am authorized to register this business for text messaging.
   - The information is accurate and matches my business records.
   - We will text only people who opted in, and only about appointments and lessons. No marketing.
   - I understand texting may carry setup and monthly fees, and I accept DanceFlow's SMS terms.
   Then choose **Submit for review**. You can use **Save draft** at any step and come back.

## What submitting does and does not do
- It records your setup and the four statements (with the time) as your consent evidence, and sends it to DanceFlow for checks.
- It does **not** contact the carriers, register anything with Twilio, buy a number, charge you, or turn texting on. Each of those is a separate step that DanceFlow takes only with its own written approval.
- Your studio will not send any text until a later, separate activation, and only to people who have opted in.

## Do not
- Email, text or paste the EIN or the form contents to DanceFlow, to Claude or to anyone else. If something looks wrong, say what is wrong without the number.
- Submit on someone else's behalf or tick a statement that is not true.

## If something fails
Note what the page says (without the EIN) and tell DanceFlow support. Do not retry repeatedly.
