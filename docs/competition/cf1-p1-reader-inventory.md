# CF1-A preflight P-1: reader inventory (heat entries and live running order)

Status: draft for CF1-A PR. Method: static read of `src/**/*.ts(x)` and `src/lib/supabase/migrations/*.sql` in worktree `studioapp-cf1-a7` (branch `cf1/a7-od13`). Nothing was executed against any database; hosted RLS/grants were NOT inspected, only what the migrations declare. Items not determinable from the repo are marked UNKNOWN.

Design reference: `callback-to-final-advancement-design.md` section 3.6 (OD-6): before official publication the generated Final roster is visible only to authorized officials and staff (managers, assigned officials, the day-of desk); competitors and the public see it only after F3 publication.

Key fact that drives every row: CF1 apply inserts the Final `event_competition_heats` row (status `scheduled`), its `event_competition_heat_entries`, and a new `event_competition_floor_heats` row directly into the LIVE (published/live) schedule version (`20270120090000_competition_cf1_callback_final_advancement.sql` lines 1274-1283, 1339-1350; draft copies via `_comp10d_copy_floor_plan` at ~1500-1560). Every reader that scopes by "published/live version" therefore sees the Final the moment apply commits, with no dependency on the callback result being published.

"Pre-publication visible?" means: could the reader show the Final heat or its entries after apply but before the callback result is published (F3).

## 5. Ungated readers

"Ungated" = could show the Final heat or its roster before the callback result is published, with gating that is NOT conditioned on callback publication state (F3):

1. S1 `competition_round_ballot_status` (chair event-wide, scorer assigned, manager): shows the Final heat and roster in the live version at apply time. Role-gated to officials/staff only. In the OD-6 audience, so permitted by design; ungated with respect to publication state and chair scope.
2. S2 `competition_judge_ballot_sheet`: shows the Final roster to any judge assigned to the Final round's panel as soon as apply commits. Role-gated; in OD-6 audience.
3. S4 `competition_round_floor_entries` (manager/chair): same, including scratched entries.
4. T1-T7 manager boards (day-of, Schedule and Heats, advanced schedule, overview counts, readiness): manager RLS only; in OD-6 audience.
5. No reader found that serves competitors or the public. This is not a proof: UNKNOWN for hosted state (extra policies/grants applied outside the repo), for the unaudited bodies noted UNKNOWN above (S7 manager check, S10 outer RPC exposure, T13, `can_manage_event_competition` arms after line 1010), and for any ARIA runtime outside `src/`.

Net: every ungated reader found is restricted to authorized officials/staff, which OD-6 allows. There is NO reader that exposes the Final to competitors or the public. The open points are the UNKNOWNs and one policy question (chair event-wide access, S1/S4).

## 6. Recommendation

Apply may be safely enabled ONLY IF all of these are closed, otherwise apply must wait for F3: (a) the UNKNOWN items above are resolved by reading the remaining function bodies and by a hosted-DB check (policies and grants on the three heat tables and the SECURITY DEFINER function list) before enabling in each environment; (b) the owner confirms chair event-wide and assigned-judge visibility of an unpublished Final is acceptable under "assigned officials"; (c) no competitor/public surface for heats or running order is added before F3. On repo evidence alone there is no competitor/public reader, so apply looks safe in the repo, but because P-1 requires "every" reader and hosted state and several definer bodies are unverified, treat it as NOT cleared until (a)-(c) are done; fallback remains apply waits for F3.
