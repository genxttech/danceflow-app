## 2. Scope and additional abbreviations

All eight source files were found in `C:\Users\mcurt\Downloads`, and each SHA-256 matched `governing-body-source-registry.md` (see `inventory-judging.md` §2.1). The two registry gaps are recorded honestly throughout: the NDCA rule book **requires** the Skating System but its text does not define the algorithm, and the WSDC rules refer to the WSDC website for Relative Placement.
