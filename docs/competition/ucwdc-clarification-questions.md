# Questions for the UCWDC Scoring Sub-Committee (DRAFT — not sent; owner sends externally)

**Prepared:** 2026-10-25, for owner review. Nothing has been sent externally.

**Source:** UCWDC Scoring Format "Heating, Scoring, & Reporting Rules", v16.9b (2026, for the 2026–2028 Rules), and the UCWDC Rules books dated 1-26-2026. The Scoring Format's General Information section names the Scoring Sub-Committee chair as the contact for questions about the format.

We are implementing the UCWDC "Majority Rules" format exactly as published, and we will not guess where the text leaves a case open. We'd be grateful for guidance on the four points below. Each one cites the clauses involved and includes a small worked example.

---

## Q1. A tie at the Semi-Finals retirement cut

**Rule 2, part 4:** "The least 'valued' contestants … (plus ties, if any occur) are retired from each Semi-Finals round until the targeted number for Finals is reached."

**Rule 2, part 3:** "the Contest Coordinator will determine the number of contestants who move on to the Finals, using the last callback values to fill the field of Finalists until the appropriate number of contestants is reached."

**Example:**
- 6 contestants, 3 judges, 1 "No" mark per judge, no alternates.
- Judge 1 marks S6 No, Judge 2 marks S5 No, Judge 3 marks S4 No.
- S4, S5 and S6 each total 10.0. The target is to retire 1.

**Question:** do all three tied contestants retire, under the "plus ties" wording of part 4? Or does the Contest Coordinator decide how many advance using the last callback values under part 3, and if so, by what rule?

---

## Q2. Counting "rounds removed" when two Preliminaries rounds are used

**Rule 9, part 1:** a contestant who did not dance a dance's Finals receives "1 placement more than the total number of Finalists for that dance, if they made all rounds up to the Finals (thus 1 round removed from the Finals)", with 2 more for 2 rounds removed, and so on.

**Rule 1, part 3, and Rule 2:** a second Preliminaries round is danced by contestants who were not promoted from the first, and Semi-Finals are used only when two or more Preliminaries rounds were danced.

**Example:**
- Prelims 1 promotes some contestants to the Semi-Finals.
- Prelims 2 (non-promoted contestants only) promotes more contestants to the Semi-Finals.
- The Semi-Finals then feed a Finals of 6.

**Question:** a contestant eliminated in Prelims 2 never danced the Semi-Finals. Are they 2 rounds removed from the Finals or 3? More generally, are "rounds" counted along the contestant's own path, or along the division's full round sequence?

---

## Q3. Rule 8 when two contestants share the best record but one beat the other directly

**Rule 8, part 2:** "The contestant with the smaller loss value number is awarded the higher placement, if a tie for the best record does not persist."

**Rule 8, part 3:** re-visitation among the remaining contestants.

**Rule 8, part 4:** a final tie for contestants "that have the same exact wins/losses/ties record after Rule #8 is executed".

**Example:** 5 judges, ProAm values. All three contestants have the identical sorted marks [12,13,13,14,15], so the tie persists through Rule 7.

| Contestant | J1 | J2 | J3 | J4 | J5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 12 | 13 | 13 | 14 | 15 |
| B | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 | 13 |
| C | 13 | 13 | 15 | 12 | 14 |

Head-to-head:
- A vs B: A 2-1-2.
- A vs C: A 2-2-1.
- B vs C: B 3-1-1.

Totals:
- A: 4-3-3, loss value 4.5.
- B: 4-3-3, loss value 4.5.
- C: 3-5-2, loss value 6.

A and B share the best record exactly, yet A beat B directly 2-1-2.

**Question:** which is correct?
- (a) A and B are in a final tie for 1st/2nd (both announced 1st, carry-forward 1.5), and C is 3rd.
- (b) C is 3rd, and A vs B is re-visited as a new two-contestant comparison, giving A 1st and B 2nd.

**A related question:** in a group of three or more, two contestants can have an equal loss value but different records, for example 13-9-0 and 11-7-4, both with a loss value of 9. Does the loss value alone decide, or does the exact record?

The same question applies to Rules 10 and 11.

## Q4. Panel eligibility and minimum number of judges

**Rule 3, part 4** defines the majority mark for odd panels and for even panels "used for contingency or emergency purposes". Its examples cover 5 to 11 judges.

We couldn't find a minimum or permitted panel size in the Scoring Format or in the 2026 Couples, ProPro/ProAm, Line Dance and Teams rule books.

**Questions:**
- What is the minimum number of judges for a UCWDC-scored Finals, and for Preliminaries and Semi-Finals?
- Are 3- and 4-judge panels permitted? Is a 4-judge panel permitted only as the "contingency or emergency" even panel?
- Which official document governs panel composition? (We'd like to cite it.)

---

## Q5. Is the Coordinator-set number of alternates a mandatory requirement?

**Rule 1, part 2** says each judge "may also be asked to additionally mark up to 5 alternates and rank them in order of preference from Alt #1 to Alt #'s 2, 3, 4, and 5", and that the Contest Coordinator may adjust the number of "Yes" marks and "Alt" ranks for a particular contest.

**Questions:**
- When the Coordinator has set a number of alternates (for example 3), must every judge mark exactly that many for the ballot to be complete?
- Or may a judge mark fewer alternates (including none) and still submit a complete, valid ballot?

Until this is answered our scoring software will treat a ballot with fewer alternates than the Coordinator-set count as incomplete and refuse it.
