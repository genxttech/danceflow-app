## Overview

Portal schedule requests let existing clients ask for a preferred lesson time from their portal.

Unless the studio sets the booking mode to Instant, this is not instant booking. The studio still reviews and approves the request before an appointment appears on the schedule.

The studio chooses the mode in Settings under Student portal scheduling: Disabled, Request only, Approval required, or Instant.

## How clients submit a request

A portal client goes to the schedule area of the client portal and completes the request form.

The request may include:

- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Lesson type
- Notes for the studio

## How staff handles the request

Staff reviews portal schedule requests from **Self-service Requests** (the **Schedule Requests** page).

A portal request is labeled separately from a public intro request so the team can understand where it came from.

## Why this is useful

Portal schedule requests reduce back-and-forth messaging while keeping the studio in control.

Clients can communicate preferred times, and staff can approve only the times that work for the instructor, room, and studio calendar.

## Best practice

Use portal schedule requests for existing clients who already understand your studio process. Keep the booking mode off Instant until your studio has clear availability rules and approval workflows in place.
