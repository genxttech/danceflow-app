# CLAUDE.md

## Competition OS: rulebook governance (permanent)

This is a permanent, non-negotiable owner directive (2026-11-22). Every Competition OS proposal, design,
implementation and review must be governed by the applicable official governing-body rulebooks (NDCA, UCWDC,
WSDC, extensible): identify the body and format, read the rule in the source itself, record source/edition/
effective date/section, distinguish mandatory vs recommended vs exception vs organizer discretion, state how it
is implemented, enforced and tested, never apply one body's rules to another without written justification, and
flag uncertain or conflicting interpretations before approval.

- No rule-dependent decision is final because a developer, reviewer or AI recommended it.
- If the rulebook is silent, label it DanceFlow policy or organizer policy; never present it as a body rule.
- Public Competition OS activation is blocked until the retrospective audit is complete and owner decisions are made.
- Do not contact competition directors or disclose unreleased Competition OS plans. Do not quote rulebooks at length.
